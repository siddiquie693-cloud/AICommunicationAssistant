Data Deletion Instructions

Effective Date: September 14, 2026

AI Communication Assistant respects your right to request deletion of your personal information and account data.

How to Request Data Deletion

To request deletion of your account and associated personal data, contact us using the contact email configured in the official developer settings for this application.

Please include:

  • The email address associated with your account.
  • The WhatsApp phone number associated with your account, if applicable.
  • A clear request to delete your account and associated data.

Do not send passwords, access tokens, API keys, or other confidential authentication credentials in your deletion request.

What Happens After Your Request

We will review the request and delete or de-identify eligible personal information associated with your account, subject to applicable legal, security, fraud-prevention, and operational requirements.

Third-Party Services

Some information may have been processed by third-party services such as Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform or AI service providers. Where applicable, deletion may also be subject to those providers' respective policies and procedures.

Processing Time

Data deletion requests will be processed within a reasonable period after the request has been verified.

Contact

For data deletion questions or requests, please use the contact email configured in the application's official developer settings.
